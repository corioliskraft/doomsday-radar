# Doomsday Radar: product vision

Doomsday Radar is a public website that looks for early signs of a regional or a global war in public data. Before such an event, the data can change: for example, fewer or more aircraft fly over a region, or many more business jets than usual fly worldwide or in a certain region, because rich people and officials who expect a crisis can leave early in private jets. On a world map, the visitor sees how many aircraft fly over a region now, compared with the same time on normal days. The map also shows where data is missing, so a gap does not look like a calm region.

Air traffic is only the first signal. More signals can follow later, for example internet outages, news volume, earthquakes, fires, and ship movements.

Start here: [walking-skeleton.md](walking-skeleton.md). The first version shows the live map with the current value against the baseline.

## Visitor

The visitor is an adult who follows news and social media and wants to check if something unusual happens now. The visitor is not an analyst, a trader, or an official.

## Why

People usually learn about a war from the news, after it has started. Public data can show signs earlier, but each source is on a different site and is hard to read for a person who is not an analyst. Doomsday Radar puts these signals on one page, in a simple form.

## Goal and success

This is a personal, non-commercial project.

Success of the first version: the live map shows the current air traffic against the baseline for any region, and it marks regions without data.

Success of the product: the site shows unusual signals before a real war starts, and false warnings are rare.
